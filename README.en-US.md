# Podcast

Turn cards, articles, and project documents into multi-speaker podcast episodes you can listen to and manage in the Podcast library, and jump back to the original source anytime.

## What It Can Do

- **One-click generation**: Choose **Generate Podcast** from the `⋯` menu of any card, article, or project document, pick a format (two-host conversation, roundtable discussion, or solo narration), speaker voices, and target length to start. AI generates the episode title automatically.
- **Generate from Notebook**: Choose **Podcast** from a Notebook's **Generate** menu and select the sources to include. AI reads the sources before writing the script, and the resulting episode is saved in the Notebook's outputs.
- **Background generation**: Progress is displayed on both the source and the Podcast library without interruption when you switch pages. If the current page is already showing the task, status updates in place. After leaving, click the notification to go straight to script review or open the finished audio.
- **Review before synthesis**: Check the option to review the script first so you can edit lines or swap speakers before synthesizing speech.
- **Podcast library**: Open **Podcast** in the Sidebar to browse all episodes. Each episode links back to its source; click the cover to play immediately while the bottom player bar keeps playing as you browse.
- **Follow along & edit lines**: In the episode view, the transcript highlights in sync with playback. Start playing from any line, or edit a single line and re-synthesize only that line.
- **Listen in the source**: Generated episodes appear above the body of the original card, article, or document so you can listen right there.

## Getting Started

1. Configure a speech service in **Settings › AI Services › Voice** (install a TTS plugin such as MiniMax Voice first).
2. On the plugin details page, open **AI Configuration**, pick a model for the podcast script, and enable it (not required when generating from a Notebook, which uses the Notebook's model).
3. Open any card, article, or project document and choose `⋯ › Generate Podcast`.

## FAQ

**Want to use your own voice?** Clone a voice in **Settings › AI Services › Voice**; it will appear under your custom voices when picking speakers.

**Is the podcast still there if I delete the source?** Yes. You can keep listening as usual, and the source will simply show as deleted.

**Do podcasts sync?** Yes. Episodes and scripts are stored in your library and sync with it; audio files are saved according to your asset storage settings.

**What if I delete an episode by mistake?** You can restore it within 7 days from **Recently Deleted** on the plugin details page.
